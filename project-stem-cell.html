Case Study · Stem Cell Banking

Banking the hope that brings life, across web & mobile

An integrated web and mobile solution for a UAE stem-cell laboratory — managing stem cell banking, donor registration, medical validation, and secure storage. Delivered in six months for the Umniati and Hortman brands.

📍 UAE🧬 Stem Cell Banking🗓️ 2024⏱️ 6 Months📱 Web & Mobile

The Challenge

Donor registration, validated by medical staff

The client needed both mobile and web applications to facilitate mother-donor registration through medical validation — streamlining umbilical-cord collection post-birth and enabling secure stem cell storage, customized per the donor's selected package.

The Solution

One platform for banking, donors & providers

In 2024, Parallax delivered an integrated system — within a six-month timeframe — that manages stem cell banking, donor information, and healthcare-provider data across a web portal and dedicated mobile apps for both the Umniati and Hortman brands.
